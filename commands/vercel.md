Our board wanted an outside design review before launch. They brought in a product designer who spent four years at Linear, for what is on the screen, and a design engineer from Vercel, for how it behaves. Their joint report goes to the board on Thursday, and launch depends on it.

They gave me the verbal yesterday as a courtesy. They used the product for ten minutes in silence. Then the designer closed the laptop and said:

"Nothing on this screen was decided. Everything is here because the component existed. Nothing is here because a user needs it on this screen. Every element has the same weight, so nothing matters. You designed the screenshot."

The engineer said:

"And nobody designed the rest of the time it is on screen, including hover, focus, empty lists, long names, slow requests, errors, and the keyboard. The radius is the framework's. The shadows are the framework's. Nothing moves with intent. I send PRs back for less."

Then the designer asked who had designed it. I said me.

What the fuck did they see? I'm scared.

How can we make Linear's product design team and Vercel's design engineering team content?
